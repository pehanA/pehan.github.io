Notes & Expository Writing

Here are some expository notes on certain pieces of fluid dynamics/applied mathematics, that I wrote up when self-teaching myself certain concepts.

  • Viscoelastic Instabilities

    These are the slides from a talk I gave at the Part III seminar series in Michaelmas term 2025. The talk explores various elastic instabilities in Non-Newtonian fluids, that occur at low Reynolds numbers. We focus on providing heuristic explanations for the physics behind the mechanisms behind these instabilities.

  • Derivation of Darcy's Law using Homogenisation

    A systematic derivation of Darcy's law for porous media flow using the method of multiple scales and asymptotic homogenisation.

  • A Proof of the Partial Converse to the Cauchy - Riemann Equations

    A set of notes, I wrote up as a second-year undergraduate, because I was never satisfied with the level of detail in any of proofs of this theorem in the recommended reading.
