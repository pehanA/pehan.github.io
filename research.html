Part III Essay: The Scallop Theorem
Supervised by Prof. Eric Lauga FRS

In this essay, we provide a detailed mathematical exposition of the scallop theorem. We start with a rigorous formulation and proof of the theorem before discussing three strategies that are often employed in biology and engineering to evade the constraints of the theorem.
