I am a first year Mathematics DPhil student at the Oxford Center for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (OCIAM), working with Professor Mohit Dalwadi and Professor Ian Griffiths .

I am funded by a Clarendon Scholarship in partnership with St John's College, Oxford.

I am broadly interested using fluid dynamical models to understand the world around us using a combination of asymptotic, numerical and data-driven methods.

Previously, I completed Part III (Applied Mathematics) at the University of Cambridge (Distinction) and completed my bachelor's degree in Mathematics with Physics at UCL (First Class Honors).
